# **App Name**: LabScape

## Core Features:

- Parallax Scrolling Sections: Implement visually engaging sections that feature parallax scrolling effects to guide users through the lab's story and research areas.
- News & Announcements Display: Feature a dedicated section for displaying the latest news, events, and announcements from the lab.
- Lab Mission & Introduction Display: Provide dedicated sections for displaying the lab's mission, research focus, introductory text, and notable quotes from figures like Asimov and Darwin.
- Lab History & Facilities Display: Showcase the lab's history through a scrollable timeline of milestones and detail its various facilities and equipment, including the Micro-chemistry, mechanical and electronic measurement laboratory (1.11G), Optical microscopy and electro-magnetic measurements laboratory (1.4A), and Cloak & dagger fabrication facility (0.016).
- Team Member Profiles: Present short biographies and photos of active and past lab members, including individual profiles and group photos, fostering a personal connection with visitors.
- Project & Research Highlights: Showcase ongoing and past individual research projects and key discoveries with brief descriptions, supporting visuals, and external links.
- Publications Management & Display: List publications sorted by year, with options for users to arrange and filter the display.
- Lab Statistics & Impact Display: Present key metrics such as the number of conducted research projects, publications, impact factor, Hirsch index, and total research funding.
- AI Content Summarizer Tool: Provide an AI tool for the lab administrator to generate concise summaries for complex research projects, publication abstracts, or team member bios from longer descriptions, aiding content creation.
- Contact & Opportunities Form: Include a simple contact form allowing visitors to send inquiries directly to the lab, and provide information on how to become a member, open positions, and proposed diploma thesis topics.
- Responsive Design & Navigation: Ensure the website is fully responsive across all devices and includes clear, intuitive navigation for all sections.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary background color: A deep, sophisticated navy blue (#0A192F) to establish the dark theme and provide a strong, professional base.
- Secondary background color: A rich, muted burgundy (#5C1A2E) for section backgrounds or prominent UI elements, adding depth and warmth to the dark theme, complementing the navy.
- Accent color: A subtle, desaturated gold or brass (#B8A281) to highlight interactive elements, text, and important information, providing an elegant contrast against the navy and burgundy tones.
- Headlines: 'Space Grotesk' (sans-serif) for a modern, tech-inspired, and commanding presence, in a light color for readability on dark backgrounds.
- Body text: 'Inter' (sans-serif) for exceptional readability and a neutral, professional tone in longer content blocks, in a light grey for comfortable reading on dark backgrounds.
- Utilize minimalist, crisp line-based icons that complement the clean, modern aesthetic, rendered in the accent color or light grey for visibility on dark backgrounds.
- Employ a modular layout with generous spacing, enhancing content readability and emphasizing key visual elements and parallax effects within the dark theme.
- Subtle, smooth scroll-triggered animations to reveal content and enhance the parallax experience without distracting from information, designed to integrate seamlessly with the dark aesthetic.
